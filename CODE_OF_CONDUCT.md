# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want Laravel Digital Menu to be a welcoming, practical, and professional open-source project. Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good intent, and keep technical discussions focused on improving the project.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- giving constructive, specific feedback;
- welcoming contributors with different experience levels;
- respecting different backgrounds, languages, and perspectives;
- keeping reviews focused on code, design, documentation, and project goals;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them professionally.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, or repeated bad-faith participation.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces where someone is participating as a member of this community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or lock contributions and conversations that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in participation restrictions.

For sensitive conduct concerns, avoid posting private information publicly. Contact the maintainer through an appropriate private channel available on their GitHub profile.

## Attribution

This policy is intentionally concise and follows the principles commonly used by healthy open-source communities.
